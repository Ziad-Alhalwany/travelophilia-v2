#### 3.2 PII Data Masking Enforcement (OWASP Top 10 Compliance)
- **Location:** [trip_requests/serializers.py](file:///d:/ZIAD%20Alhalwany/TRAVILOPHILIA/TRAVELOPHILIA%20WEBSITE/_worktrees/qa/backend/django_api/trip_requests/serializers.py)
- **Masking Mechanism:**
  - Raw national ID or passport numbers passed during booking submission (`leader_identity_number`) are declared as `write_only=True` in `TripRequestCreateSerializer`. They are never returned in public or CRM API responses.
  - In `Customer.save()`, raw identity values are parsed to extract only the final 4 digits into `identity_last4` (e.g., `****1234`).
  - Cryptographic one-way hashing (`make_password(raw_id)`) is stored in `identity_hash` for unique customer identity verification without storing plain text PII.
